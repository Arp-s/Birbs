# Lyall's wren Journal

**Lyall's wren Journal** is a web journal dedicated to (scientific) ecology, ethology and evolution. Its main objective is to offer an online journal that presents biological information that is sourced, accessible, and understandable to all.

## Objectives
- **Scientific mediation**: Popularize concepts and discoveries in biology and ecology.
- **Scientific news**: Present the new studies and knowledge on those topics.
- **Reliable sources**: All published information is accompanied by scientific references.
- **Accessibility**: Make science accessible to the general public, regardless of their level of knowledge.

## Features
- Thematic articles on ecology and ethology
- Articles on misunderstood concepts or problematic popularization
- Scientific news sections
- Information on ecosystems, ecosystem dynamics, wildlife interaction
- Bibliography and links to external resources

## Founder
The founder is currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in biology, with a focus on ecosystem ecology, currently in there third year.
They are a particular interest in the interactions within large animal groups, such as bird flocks, fish schools, and animal societies like ants, naked mole rats, and meerkats.They are more likely to write about wildlife. Their aim is to deliberately compensate for the bias in knowledge about birds and mammals, by more talking about "reptiles", amphibians, "fishes", arthropods, mollusca, and all other taxonomy classes. 
They writing may reflect certain biases, including an anti-speciesist perspective and a preference for minimizing disturbance to wildlife, plant life and microfauna.
They are French speaker, so they may make mistakes in English, sorry about that.
